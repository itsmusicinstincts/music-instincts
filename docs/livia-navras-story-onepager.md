# Maya & the Nine Rasas — Story One-Pager

*A 20–25 min comical, theatrical Diwali stage show — live dance, Bollywood song snippets, video backdrop, and a social message, built around the ancient Navras (nine emotions).*

---

## Logline

An AI humanoid named **Maya**, built by a scientist (**AKV**), can scan and analyze the whole world — but feels nothing. When the mythic, wisecracking **Captain Karma** shows up (summoned, as always, the instant someone says "instant karma"), he takes on the impossible task of teaching a machine the **Nav Ras** — the nine flavours of the human heart — before the night is over. Each rasa she truly learns breaks away one piece of the mask hiding her face. To teach her, Karma turns to a real, ongoing story unfolding among the LIVIA condo community: **The Sport Seekers**, a cycling/sports team whose friendships, jealousies, and courage play out the emotions live in front of her — and the audience.

---

## Tone & Social Message

- **Comic, warm, never preachy.** Karma is a playful trickster (terrible puns, deep wisdom underneath), not a punishing god.
- **"Instant karma"** — consequences shown immediately and visibly, for laughs, not lectures.
- Underlying message: **an AI can learn to imitate every human expression — but karma (conscience, consequence, connection) is what actually makes those expressions mean something.** Humans feel because they carry that invisible thread with each other; a machine can only borrow the shape of it.

## Cast of Characters

| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| **Maya** | The AI humanoid. Precise, literal, unintentionally hilarious ("Laddoos: downloaded"). Wears a nine-piece mask that breaks away as she learns; ends the show with a full human face. |
| **AKV** | The scientist who built Maya. Opens the show, narrates between acts. |
| **Captain Karma** | Cosmic showman and "Chief Accountant" of karma. Appears whenever summoned. Teaches Maya the nine rasas. Terrible shayari, great comic timing. |
| **Kool** | Karma's assistant — the agent of *positive* karma. Nudges people toward kindness (often literally, like a proton). |
| **PratiKool** | Karma's other assistant — the agent of *negative* karma (the "electron"). Instigates jealousy, mischief, and mess for comic conflict. |
| **Audit Rani** | Keeps the cosmic ledger. Deadpan comic relief; announces the "balance sheet" of good and bad deeds. |
| **Satak Lee** | Coach of The Sport Seekers. Exasperated, well-meaning, sports-anthem energy. |
| **Karan** | Co-lead of the Sport Seekers story. Talented, easily distracted, falls for Naina. |
| **Arjun** | Karan's best friend and rival. Feels sidelined, gets manipulated by PratiKool into scheming — with unintended, painful consequences. |
| **Naina** | Karan's love interest; catalyst for the Shringar (love) rasa within the Sport Seekers arc. |
| **The Ensemble** | The LIVIA community — dancers, singers, the full team, featured throughout. |

## The Story, Beat by Beat

**Setup**
1. **AKV's Creation Video** — Maya wakes up, brilliant and empty ("Emotions: not found").
2. **Maya scans the world** — she detects everything at the Diwali celebration (laddoos, rangoli, fireworks) and feels nothing.
3. **"Instant Karma!"** — a Diwali mishap on screen triggers the phrase, and Captain Karma bursts onto the stage, delighted with his own entrance.
4. Karma proposes the deal: learn all nine rasas, one mask piece breaks away for each.

**Rasa 1 — Shringar (Love), Maya's own lesson**
5. Maya scans her own scanner, Murali (named after Krishna's flute), and relives Radha & Krishna's love and longing — set to a flute-led romantic number (*Hawa Ke Saath Saath*). First mask piece breaks away.

**Karma's transition** — "Chalo, let's look at real lives..." — Title card: **THE SPORT SEEKERS**

6. Coach Satak Lee's assessment: the team isn't ready — too many individual egos.
7. Karan & Arjun's friendship intro ("All Is Well").
8. Basketball vs. the girls' team — Karan pulls focus, Arjun feels sidelined (*Yeh Ladka Hai Deewana*).
9. **The Fork** — PratiKool instigates Arjun's jealousy; Kool nudges Karan toward Naina.
10. **Shringar (Love)** — Karan & Naina's spark (*Kuch Kuch Hota Hai* sting → *Main Hoon Na*).
11. Kool pitches a team party to the coach, hoping fun brings the team together.
12. **Hasya (Laughter)** — The Qawwali Party: a joyful, chaotic contest with Kool awarding bonus points and PratiKool docking them for laughs.
13. Jealousy surfaces in the group, on stage and on screen.
14. **Bibhatsa (Disgust)** — Arjun plots to get Karan disqualified from the big race, and takes over as team leader, self-justifying: "I'm not distracted by girls."
15. **The Accident** — the plot backfires: Arjun's own sister is endangered, and Karan gets badly hurt protecting her.
16. **Raudra (Anger)** — the whole team is furious about what happened to Karan; Arjun hides his guilt.
17. **Karuna (Sorrow)** — Karan's injury and the team's quiet grief.
18. **Veera (Courage)** — Kool redirects the team's anger into supporting Karan's recovery (gym, swim, run); the team reconciles and reprises *Hawa Ke Saath Saath* on race day, courage earned together.

**Finale**
19. **Adbhuta (Wonder)** — the full company comes together in one finale song/medley, all nine rasas flashing at once. Maya realizes: this coming-together *is* the wonder — how humans hold every emotion at once, together.
20. **Shanta / Closing Twist** — Maya feels complete... until Karma gently tells her: "You collected the faces. You didn't collect the karma." The one true difference between her and the humans she now understands.

## Format Notes

- **Runtime:** ~20–25 min (trim dance/song lengths to fit; full outline can run longer if expanded).
- **Songs:** Bollywood snippets used for comedy, romance, and dance numbers (e.g. *Hawa Ke Saath Saath*, *Yeh Ladka Hai Deewana*, *Kuch Kuch Hota Hai* / *Main Hoon Na*, a qawwali number, an original Karma hook song).
- **Visuals:** Live stage + video backdrop; Maya's nine-piece mask breaks away piece by piece and reassembles on a lit stand, visible to the audience all night.
- **Costumes:** Bright, colour-coded by rasa; Maya's robo-dress with LED accents; Karma in gold/saffron mythic styling.
